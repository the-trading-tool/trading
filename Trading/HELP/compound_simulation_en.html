Compound simulation compound_simulation.py

Computes the real (inflation-adjusted) growth of a lump-sum investment – either as a forward projection or based on historical interest-rate and inflation data.

Modes

ModeData sourceUse
ProjectionCurrent TNX yield (10-year US Treasuries) + current CPI inflation"What if I invest today?"
HistoricalMonthly TNX and CPI time series from Yahoo FinanceRetrospective: actual real return of past years

Inputs

ParameterMeaning
Starting capitalOne-off invested amount in the system currency
Term (years)Investment horizon for the projection
Interest rateManually overridable; default = current TNX value
Inflation rateManually overridable; default = current CPI value

Result

  • Nominal curve – growth without deducting inflation
  • Real curve – purchasing-power-adjusted growth
  • Final value and loss of purchasing power in absolute figures
Reachable via the Lump sum investment menu item in the left navigation bar.
