Asset Viewer: Scorecard ticker_scorecard_page.py

If the ticker is an Edge Scores market (index, commodity future, currency, crypto), the tab shows the asset scorecard. For single stocks there are three components from local daily prices:

ComponentRangeRule
Trend & seasonality−4 … +4as in the trend card: price vs SMA200, SMA50 vs SMA200, 3-month momentum, seasonality of the next 30 days
Relative strength−2 … +213-week return minus index return: ±1 from ±2 pp, ±2 from ±10 pp
Market environment−10 … +10total score of the index (for indices = trend)

Total = 10 × mean of the normalised components (trend /4, RS /2, market /10). Bias from ±2, strong from ±5; the same brakes as in the Edge Scores (price confirmation via trend and SMA200, cap with only one component). The equal weighting is not measured – this describes the situation, it is not a trading signal.

Context, not in the score: risk bucket, trend score, 4PS phase, Markov regime and Minervini RS from the latest simulation row, plus the macro score of the listing currency. None of these showed a return edge in our data; fundamentals are left out on purpose (no history, look-ahead).

History and change are causal: each weekly point uses only prices known by then.
