Earnings calendar earnings_calendar.py

Shows upcoming and past earnings releases of the monitored companies with EPS estimates and actual values.

Columns

ColumnMeaning
TickerSymbol of the security
DateScheduled or actual earnings date
EPS EstimatedAnalyst consensus estimate (earnings per share)
EPS ActualActually reported EPS (only after release)
Surprise %Deviation of the reported EPS from consensus in percent

Filters

  • Period: next 7 / 14 / 30 days or past quarters
  • Index filter: show only names of a specific index

Usage note

Earnings releases often cause strong price moves. Open positions in the company concerned should be reviewed before the earnings date – adjust the stop-loss or close the position if needed.

Data comes from Yahoo Finance and may update with a delay. For time-critical decisions, always check the original source (the company's investor relations).
The calendar is available as a tab on the Asset Details page and additionally via the main menu item Earnings Calendar.
