Dashboard banner_page.py

The app's start page. It gives an overview of the overall portfolio, current AI trading tips and the historical strategy performance.

Section overview

SectionContent
Metrics rowStarting capital · Current portfolio value · Performance (%) · Maximum positions
Strategy labelsActive trading strategy/strategies and their associated indices
AI trading tipAutomatically generated analysis of the most recently bought asset (Groq / Gemini)
Trades tableAll open and closed positions with profit/loss
Cumulative gain chartTime series of the realised total profit
Strategy metricsProfit factor, hit rate, avg. profit/trade per strategy
Monthly heatmapRdYlGn colour coding: gains (green) vs. losses (red) per month/year
Portfolio overlap warningNotice when an asset is active in several strategies at once
DisclaimerLegal notice (collapsed)

Language selection

Top left: a dropdown between Deutsch and English. The choice is stored in config.db and persists across sessions.

Reading the strategy metrics

MetricMeaning
Profit factorSum of gains ÷ sum of losses (value > 1 = profitable)
Hit rateShare of profitable trades in %
Avg. profit/tradeAverage realised profit per completed trade
Realised profitTotal sum of all closed positions
Longest winning streakMaximum number of consecutive winning trades
The AI tip is generated automatically when run_agent.py or asset_perf2.py processes a new buy signal. No API key → no tip.
