What is the OVT?

The overallValueTrend is a proprietary composite indicator that combines multiple categories of evaluation criteria:

  • Trend strength: daily, weekly and monthly trend direction, MACD, RSI, ADX, EWO, momentum
  • Volatility measures: log volatility, volatility relative to price movement
  • Fundamentals: ROA, P/E ratio (forward & trailing), earnings growth, dividend yield, operating margins, P/B ratio, debt level
  • Market sentiment: analyst price targets, average trading volume, Sharpe ratio
  • Machine learning: XGBoost price forecast as an additional filter signal

Each criterion contributes a partial score and a weight. The OVT is the weighted average of all contributions, normalised to the −100 to +100 scale.

My trading strategy with the OVT

Buy signal

  • A stock's OVT is at least above the index average
  • The EWO (Elliott Wave Oscillator) is rising and above its EMA (period 9)
  • Optionally confirmed by at least one overlay or oscillator indicator to reduce false positives

Sell signal

  • The EWO falls below its 9-period EMA, or
  • The OVT drops below the defined threshold

Portfolio construction

  • 3–8 stocks per index (SPX, GDAXI, MDAXI, SDAX, etc.) for broad risk diversification
  • Cover as many indices as possible — up to ~40 positions simultaneously
  • Stop-loss: half the stock's average volatility (typically around −3 % of the buy price)
  • Trail the stop-loss upward daily (ATR trailing stop)
  • Holding period: a few days to several weeks — no day-trading
All price data comes from Yahoo Finance and is delivered with a slight delay. This is perfectly adequate for the strategy — it does not require tick-level precision.

Combining OVT with other indicators

The OVT is a powerful signal, but it works best as part of a broader setup. False positives can be significantly reduced by pairing the OVT with additional indicators available in the app:

  • Overlay: Heikin-Ashi candles, Bollinger Bands, Support/Resistance
  • Oscillator: EWO (primary sell signal), RSI, MACD histogram

Rule of thumb: buy when OVT ≥ threshold and at least one additional indicator confirms the direction.
