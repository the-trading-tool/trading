Edge Scores: Macro Scorecard macro_page.py

The Macro Scorecard (Market → Edge Scores, sub-menu on the left) rates the eight major currencies by whether their economic data pushes the central bank towards higher or lower rates – and thereby supports or weighs on the currency. Result: a score from −6 to +6 per currency and a bias per forex pair.

Data sources (free, no API key)

IndicatorSourceFrequency

Leading indicator CLIOECD; EUR via the four large EU economiesmonthly, not for CHF/NZD
  • The page refreshes at most once a day when opened (the Refresh button in the sub-menu forces it). CLI/scheduler: python -m tradinglib.macro_data.
  • Stored in database/macro.db, history from 2015. If one source fails, the others stay current; the page names the affected source.

Score per currency

ComponentRulePoints
RatesPolicy rate above/below the median of the 8 currencies; plus raised/cut vs. 6 months ago±1 each
Inflationy/y rate vs. 3 months ago > +0.2 pp / < −0.2 pp±1
Labour marketUnemployment rate vs. 3 months ago fell / rose (> 0.1 pp)±1
GrowthGDP q/q ≥ 0.5 % / < 0 %±1
Leading indicatorCLI > 100 and rising / < 100 and falling±1

For quarterly series the change is measured against the prior quarter. A missing value scores 0. Pair score = base − quote (−12 … +12), bias from ±3, strong from ±6.

MethodologyShort version of this help.
The score rates the direction of the data, not the surprise versus forecasts (consensus estimates are not freely available). Macro data arrives with weeks to months of delay – the score describes the backdrop, not timing. Thresholds are a first approximation, not backtest-calibrated. Not investment advice.
