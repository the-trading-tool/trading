Edge Scores: Asset Scorecard asset_card_page.py

The asset scorecard (Market → Edge Scores → Asset scorecard) shows one market with all building blocks of the Edge Scores on one page: total score with gauge, the three components with their individual points, technical context and the history of the total score.

Price confirmation

If the trend score or the price's position vs the SMA200 points against the direction of the total score, the bias is neutral (note “price does not confirm” in the banner). Example bitcoin 11/2026 (still with COT in the score): COT +2, trend +1 (3-month momentum positive from the August low) → total +4.6, but the price is below the SMA200 and has been falling for two months – so neutral instead of bullish.

Only one component

Macro data exist only for the eight major currencies and COT does not count; for all other markets the total score is therefore the trend alone. So that trend +3 (→ +7.5) does not look as “strong” as three aligned components, the bias is capped at Bullish/Bearish in that case (note “trend only” in the banner). This also applies in the Total score card.

History without look-ahead

Each weekly point uses only what was known that day: COT from its release (Friday), trend on prices up to that day, macro only with values estimated to have been published (policy rate/leading indicator ≈ 42 days, inflation/unemployment ≈ 70 days after the start of the month, GDP ≈ 120 days after the start of the quarter). Today's point is identical to the individual cards.

Weekly points are read from the stored history (edge_scores.db, job EDGE_SCORES_WEEKLY, Sat/Sun 12:00), which applies the same rules. Only today's point and weeks still missing there are computed live; the caption shows the counts.

Not included because there is no free source: crowd/retail sentiment, consensus forecasts (“surprise”) and price targets. The scores describe the situation; the thresholds are not yet calibrated by backtest.
