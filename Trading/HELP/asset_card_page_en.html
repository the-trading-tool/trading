Edge Scores: Asset Scorecard asset_card_page.py

The asset scorecard (Market → Edge Scores → Asset scorecard) shows one market with all building blocks of the Edge Scores on one page: total score with gauge, the three components with their individual points, technical context and the history of the total score.

Layout

HistoryTotal score per Friday (26/52/104 weeks)–

History without look-ahead

Each weekly point uses only what was known that day: COT from its release (Friday), trend on prices up to that day, macro only with values estimated to have been published (policy rate/leading indicator ≈ 42 days, inflation/unemployment ≈ 70 days after the start of the month, GDP ≈ 120 days after the start of the quarter). Today's point is identical to the individual cards.

Not included because there is no free source: crowd/retail sentiment, consensus forecasts (“surprise”) and price targets. The scores describe the situation; the thresholds are not yet calibrated by backtest.
