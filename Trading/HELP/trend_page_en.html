Edge Scores: Trend & Seasonality trend_page.py

The Trend & Seasonality card (Market → Edge Scores, sub-menu on the left) rates the 20 markets of the COT Scorecard plus 16 further equity indices (DAX, FTSE, Nikkei, KOSPI, Bovespa …) and 7 further cryptocurrencies by their price trend and the typical behaviour of the coming month. Result: a score from −4 to +4 per market and a bias per forex pair.

Data

  • Daily closes from the app's local price databases (yf_<ticker>.db, read-only).
  • If a series is missing or older than 7 days it is loaded directly from Yahoo – without writing to the databases (currently e.g. NZDUSD and WTI).
  • FX rates vs. USD are partly derived from the cross rate vs. EUR × EURUSD (e.g. JPY, CHF, CAD, AUD).
  • The 10y Treasury is measured via its yield ^TNX and the direction flipped (⇅).
  • The global indices and further cryptos are not part of the total score – there is no COT or macro data for them.

Score per market

ComponentRulePoints
Long-term trendClose above / below SMA200±1
Trend structureSMA50 above / below SMA200±1
MomentumReturn over the last 63 trading days > +1 % / < −1 %±1
SeasonalityComing month (30 calendar days from today) over the last 10 years: hit rate ≥ 70 % and positive average / ≤ 30 % and negative average (at least 7 years)±1

Seasonality deliberately counts only ±1 so that a statistic from 10 values does not override a clear trend. Pair score = base − quote (−8 … +8), bias from ±3, strong from ±5.
