Edge Scores: COT Scorecard cot_page.py

The COT Scorecard (Market → Edge Scores, sub-menu on the left) shows how large speculators (funds, CTAs – "non-commercials" in CFTC terms) are positioned in currencies, commodities, equity indices, bonds and Bitcoin, condensed into a score from −3 to +3 per market and a bias per forex pair.

Data source

  • CFTC Commitments of Traders, dataset "Legacy – futures only", via the free CFTC Public Reporting API (no API key).
  • Data is as of Tuesday and released the following Friday.
  • The page refreshes automatically once a new report is due. Manually: Refresh button or via CLI/scheduler python -m tradinglib.cot_data (/full = full history).
  • Stored in database/cot.db, history from 2018.

Score per market

ComponentRulePoints
PositioningLong share = long / (long + short) of non-commercials, measured as the rank over the last 3 years of this market (column “Rank 3y”, 0 = lowest, 100 = highest): ≥ 80 / ≥ 60 / ≤ 40 / ≤ 20+2 / +1 / −1 / −2
MomentumChange of the long share vs. prior week: > +1 pp / < −1 pp+1 / −1

Why relative? In gold and silver speculators are almost always net long (silver 2018–2026 median 69 %), in the yen mostly short. Fixed thresholds such as “≥ 60 % long = bullish” rated such markets almost always the same – even while speculators were cutting positions (silver 11/2026). The rank shows whether positioning is currently high or low for this market.

COT index (not in the score): net position between the min and max of the last 52 weeks (0–100). ≥ 90 or ≤ 10 is flagged as crowded.
