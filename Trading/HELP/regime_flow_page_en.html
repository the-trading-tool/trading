Regime Flow regime_flow_page.py

A market-breadth dashboard that classifies hundreds of assets simultaneously into three market regimes (Bull, Bear, Sideways) and visualises their evolution over time, capital flows and sector shifts.

Quick overview – tabs

TabContent
🌊 TidalStacked area chart: percentage share of assets in Bull/Bear/Sideways over time
🔀 Capital FlowSankey diagram of regime transitions + mini Sankeys per sector
🗺 MigrationBubble scatter: sector shifts in the Bull-vs-Bear space with arrows
📊 Flow TableSingle-name table with Flow Score, relative volume and EWO

A clear decline of the green area together with a rise of the red one signals a market rotation early, before it noticeably hits indices.

Tab 2 – 🔀 Capital Flow

Main Sankey

Shows how many assets changed regime. The node width corresponds to the asset count, the flow width is additionally weighted by the mean relative volume (relvol_ratio).

SideMeaning
Left nodesRegime at the start of the interval (Bull / Bear / Sideways)
Right nodesRegime today
Flow colourColour of the source regime (green / red / grey)

Example: a thick green flow from Bull → Bear means that many assets that started bullish are now bearish – and at above-average volume.

Mini Sankeys per sector

The same transition logic, but isolated per sector, in a 2-column grid. It enables comparison: which sector loses more, which one holds up?

If the interval is too short for comparison data, the dashboard shows only the current regime distribution (without transition arrows).

Bubble scatter plot with one point per sector.

Y-axisBear share of the sector (0–100 %)

ArrowsFrom previous period → today; appear only for a shift of >1 percentage point

Reading the quadrants

QuadrantLabelInterpretation
Bottom rightOpportunityHigh Bull share, low Bear share → stable uptrend
Top rightVolatileMany Bull and Bear assets at once → mixed, nervous picture
Top leftDangerLow Bull, high Bear share → downward pressure dominates
Bottom leftStagnationNeither Bull nor Bear → the market is treading water
Arrows show the direction of the shift. A sector whose arrow points from "Danger" to "Opportunity" is in early recovery mode.

Indicators in detail

Markov regime classification

The basis for all regime assignments. The logarithmic 20-bar return is computed:

RegimeCondition
▲ Bulllog(Close / Close[–20]) > +5 %
▼ Bearlog(Close / Close[–20]) < −5 %
─ Sideways–5 % ≤ return ≤ +5 %

Flow Score

Rates the strength of the regime move per name:

Flow Score = regime direction × Rel.Vol.Ratio × (1 + |normalised EWO angle|)

FactorValue
Regime direction+1 (Bull), −1 (Bear), 0 (Sideways)
Rel.Vol.RatioVolume / 20-day avg, capped to [0.1 … 10.0]
Normalised EWO angleEWO (capped to [−10, +10]) ÷ 10, absolute value
A high positive Flow Score means: the name is bullish and trades at above-average volume with positive momentum. A deep negative value signals bears under pressure with elevated activity.

Relative volume (Rel.Vol.)

Indicates whether a name is being actively traded compared to its own 20-day average.

  • > 1.5: clearly elevated volume – institution or news?
  • 0.8–1.5: normal range
  • < 0.8: below-average activity – signal less reliable

EWO (Elliott Wave Oscillator)

Measures momentum as the distance between two EMAs:

EWO % = (EMA5 − EMA35) / Close × 100

  • EWO > 0 & rising (↗): the short-term trend is accelerating
  • EWO > 0 & falling (↘): momentum divergence – caution with longs
  • EWO < 0 & falling (↘): downward pressure is increasing

Market-stress panel

The banner below the 5 metrics rates the overall market state based on a 10-day rolling window.

Stress components

ComponentWeightMeaning
bull_drop28 %Falling slope of the Bull share (pp/day)
bear_rise24 %Rising slope of the Bear share (pp/day)
divergence20 %Index stable or rising although Bull breadth falls – strongest warning signal
side_rise14 %Increasing sideways movement (indecision)
relvol14 %Elevated relative volume across all names (nervousness)

Stress levels

ScoreLevelRecommended action
≥ 60⚠️ WarningReview hedges, reduce position sizes
33–59🔶 ElevatedIncreased vigilance, scrutinise new entries
< 33🟢 CalmNormal market environment

Recovery score (counterpart)

Signals a possible bottoming after a sell-off phase:

ComponentWeightMeaning
bull_rise45 %Rising Bull breadth from lows
bear_drop30 %Falling Bear share – selling pressure eases
relvol25 %Elevated volume on the buyer side
  • Score ≥ 60: "Turning" – possible bottom
  • Score 33–59: "Building" – first signs of recovery
