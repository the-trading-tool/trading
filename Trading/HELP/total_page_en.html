Edge Scores: Total Score total_page.py

The Total Score (Market → Edge Scores, sub-menu on the left) combines the three scorecards COT positioning, macro data and trend & seasonality into one value from −10 to +10 per market and derives the top setups for forex pairs.

Calculation

ComponentRangeNormalisationavailable for
COT positioning−3 … +3÷ 3all 20 markets
Macro data−6 … +6÷ 68 major currencies
Trend & seasonality−4 … +4÷ 4all 20 markets

Total = 10 × average of the available normalised components (equally weighted). Bias from ±2, strong from ±5. Aligned (✓) = all available components point the same way and none is neutral.

Tabs

TabContent
Top setupsForex pairs by strength (base − quote, −20 … +20) with direction and the difference per component; aligned pairs only by default.
OverviewAll markets grouped with total, bias, alignment and the three sub-scores.
MethodologyShort version of this help.
The sub-scores update at different speeds (COT weekly, macro monthly to quarterly, trend daily). A bias filter, not an entry signal, not backtest-calibrated. Not investment advice.
