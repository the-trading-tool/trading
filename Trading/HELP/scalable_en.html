Analyse your Scalable Capital portfolio

This app reads your transaction export from Scalable Capital and turns it into a full portfolio analysis: open positions with their current price, realised and unrealised gains, dividends, interest, fees and cashflow — plus charts, a market overview, sector rotation and a lump-sum investment simulation. All free of charge.

Get started in 3 steps

1. Get the export from Scalable. In the Scalable Capital app: Account → Transactions → Export CSV. You'll receive a .csv file containing all your bookings.
2. Upload it. This app opens on the Own Transactions page. Open the Scalable Capital tab there and upload the CSV. Only rows with status Executed are processed — cancelled orders are ignored.
3. Automatic detection. Every ISIN in your export is automatically matched to the right exchange ticker, and the corresponding price and reference data are loaded in the background (source: Yahoo Finance, with FMP as a fallback). No manual mapping required.
Privacy: Your analysis is protected by your login. Uploaded portfolio data is visible only to you — other users cannot see it.

What the app shows you

AreaContent
Own Transactions Open positions with average buy price, current price, market value, weight and profit/loss. Plus a cashflow overview, dividends, interest, fees and taxes from your Scalable export. Click a position to open its chart with your purchase line.
Asset viewer Detailed price chart for each instrument with technical indicators (overlays and oscillators) you can configure yourself.
Market overview Global market indicators (volatility, rates, lead indices, commodities, crypto) at a glance, with an optional AI assessment.
Sector rotation Which sectors are currently attracting or losing capital — shown as a rotation graph and heatmap.
Lump-sum investment Simulation of a one-off investment with compound interest and inflation adjustment — forecast or historical values.

Re-importing & corrections

Each import adds new rows without checking for existing ones. Uploading the same CSV twice creates duplicates. Use the Delete trades section on the Scalable page to remove affected rows again (Scalable imports only, or all of them).

Note: This app is not investment advice. Prices may be delayed. All analyses are for information purposes only.
