Sector Rotation sector_rotation_page.py

A three-tier dashboard for analysing capital flows between market sectors – from the macro overview down to the single-stock drill-down.

Tabs

TabContent
RRG WeeklyRelative Rotation Graph (weekly) of all sectors versus the reference index
RRG DailyRelative Rotation Graph (daily) – a shorter-term perspective
TreemapCapital-flow treemap: tile size = market weight, colour = relative strength
Sector MatrixTabular comparison of all sectors with RSC, CMF and OBV
Industry Drill-downSelect a sector → industry detail with the strongest single stocks
Best of SectorTop names per sector by RSC vs. ETF enrichment

Reading the RRG

QuadrantPhaseMeaning
Top rightLeadingStronger than the market and still increasing → overweight
Bottom rightWeakeningStronger than the market, but momentum fading → watch
Bottom leftLaggingWeaker than the market and still decreasing → avoid
Top leftImprovingWeaker than the market, but momentum turning → consider entry

Indicators used

  • RSC – Relative Strength Comparison (vs. benchmark ETF)
  • CMF – Chaikin Money Flow (capital in-/outflow)
  • OBV – On Balance Volume (cumulative volume direction)

Choosing the universe

At the top of the dashboard: US (S&P 500 sectors), EU (STOXX 600 sectors) or EM (Emerging Markets). The chosen universe determines the sectors, ETFs and reference index.

The page is reachable via the Sector Rotation menu item or directly via /?rotation=true.
